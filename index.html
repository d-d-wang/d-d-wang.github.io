About Me

Building Data-Efficient and Reliable AI Tools That Sense the World

I obtained my Ph.D. in computer science in University of Central Florida, advised by Drs. Liqiang Wang and Boqing Gong. My research focuses on data-efficient and reliable artificial intelligence, spanning machine learning, computer vision, and natural language processing. I am particularly interested in advancing AI methods with applications in intelligent transportation systems, urban sustainability, and public health. Prior to pursuing my Ph.D., I earned a Master's degree in Environmental Science from Duke University, where I focused on numerical modeling and scientific computing. Following my Ph.D., I joined the Smart & Safe Transportation Lab at the University of Central Florida, led by Dr. Mohamed Abdel-Aty, where I served as a Postdoctoral Scholar and Machine Learning Lead, guiding research projects, mentoring Ph.D. students, and advancing AI-driven transportation safety systems.
